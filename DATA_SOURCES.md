# Data sources and terms of use

## Dataset scope and provenance

The project combines two public datasets from Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS): the Labour Force Survey 2024–25 and Population and Housing Census 2023. The team downloaded existing official releases; it did not conduct either original data collection. The LFS supplies individual and household attributes and the employment label. Census Table 1 supplies regional population density, urban proportion and household size. Supporting documents and reference publications below are not additional individual-level datasets.

The modelling export contains 26,653 labour-force youth aged 15–24 and 19 predictor features, plus the target and a household grouping key. Counts are recorded in `reports/tables/row_waterfall.csv`; the predictor allowlist is `src/hunarlens/config.py::FEATURES`. Census indicators are joined using province and area, not individual or district matching. The intended users are students, researchers and workforce-policy analysts exploring aggregate patterns; this is not an individual hiring or eligibility tool.

The processed model export excludes names, addresses, phone numbers and original person identifiers through an explicit column allowlist. Its numeric `group_id` supports household-aware splitting and is not a predictor or a claim of complete anonymisation. Raw, intermediate and person-level processed files are excluded from Git; small aggregate tables and app reference lookups are retained. Licence uncertainty is preserved in the source-specific status below.

## Pakistan Bureau of Statistics data

The [PBS data-dissemination page](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/data-dissemination/) describes non-confidential tables, publications and metadata as Tier 1, and anonymized public-use files as Tier 2. It permits copying, adaptation and reuse of Tier 1/2 products with attribution. An older [data-dissemination policy](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Data-Dissemination-Policy.pdf) also restricts onward supply of data obtained under that policy. The descriptions below apply the published categories where appropriate; they do not establish a file-specific licence or override any conditions supplied with a file.
