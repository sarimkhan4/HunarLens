Definitions that matter

Youth

For the official LFS headline used here: people aged 15–24. The prototype estimator accepts ages 15–24.

Unemployed

Not employed, available within two weeks, and either searching in the previous four weeks or having a job to start soon.

Unemployment rate

Unemployed people as a share of the labour force—not a share of all young people.

Participation rate

People in the labour force as a share of the relevant population. It includes employed and unemployed people.

Important limitations and responsible use

Read before interpreting or sharing results
  • Survey indicators are population statistics. They do not describe the certainty or circumstances of any individual.
  • Estimates use the project?s frozen model and the same input validation as the original app.
  • The model analysis uses unweighted records, which can affect representativeness.
  • Students who are not working may be outside the labour-force sample, affecting comparisons involving enrolment.
  • Reported model evaluation comes from one held-out test split. It is not evidence of ongoing monitoring, external validation or future performance.
  • Before production deployment, add survey weights where appropriate, subgroup confidence intervals, temporal validation, calibration checks, model versioning, privacy review and independent fairness assessment.
